What he does

Keeps an eye on Claude

He watches claude.ai in your browser, the Claude app and Claude Code, and acts it out: typing away while Claude works, waving when it's waiting on you, celebrating when it's done.

Chats with you

Double-click him and ask anything. He can search the web, find files in the folders you choose, and check your Gmail and Calendar through your Claude connectors.

Checks in each morning

The first time you sit down, he gives you a short rundown: today's meetings and the emails that look like they need you.

Asks before he changes anything

Drafting an email or adding a calendar event shows you exactly what he'll do, with Yes and No. He never sends email, and he tells you when there's a new version of him.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 12 Monterey or later. Apple silicon and Intel both work.
  • Claude Pro or Max. His chat runs on Claude Code, which comes with those plans. On the free plan he still reacts to Claude, but he can't chat.
  • That's it. He installs Claude Code and signs you in himself, so there's no Terminal involved.

Where your stuff goes

Your chats with him go to Anthropic through Claude Code, under your own Claude account, the same as chatting on claude.ai. Gmail and Calendar go through your claude.ai connectors.

Nothing else leaves your Mac. There are no analytics, no accounts and no server of ours. Once a day he asks GitHub whether there's a newer version, and that request has nothing about you in it.

Install him

  1. Download the DMG and open it.
  2. Drag Fox Buddy into Applications.
  3. Open him and click Set Up, then answer a few quick questions so he knows what to call you.

Or, with Homebrew:

brew tap justinfuchs85/fox-buddy https://github.com/justinfuchs85/fox-buddy
brew install --cask fox-buddy
